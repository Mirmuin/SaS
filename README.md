## Description

**Space & Steel: Industry** is a groundbreaking technological modpack for version 1.7.10, inviting players to undertake a grand journey of advancement from a primitive campfire to the construction of a Dyson Sphere. Tailored for a deep survival playthrough, it challenges your engineering skills with immensely complex automation chains, NTM's radiation hazards, and a space-faring endgame—with each successive stage building upon and expanding the previous ones.

## Capabilities

*   Ore enrichment through multiple processing methods
*   Resource extraction using customizable handheld and stationary drills
*   Full production automation
*   Numerous energy generation methods:
    *   Thermal power plants (coal, gas, liquid)
    *   Nuclear power plants (RBMK, PWR, Zirnox, etc.)
    *   Hydroelectric power plants
    *   Windmills
    *   Solar panels
    *   Geothermal power plants
    *   Fusion power plants
*   Customizable rockets for spaceflight — or total annihilation
*   Nuclear bombs
*   Other planets with their own unique resource ecosystems
*   Aircraft, tanks, helicopters, naval fleet, trains
*   Cannons, muskets, pistols, machine guns, rocket launchers, blasters, and more
